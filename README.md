### Business questions explored

- How are revenue and profit distributed across brands, categories and countries?
- Which country contributes the largest customer base and sales volume?
- Which payment methods are most frequently used?
- How does order volume change across years?
- How consistent are shipping costs across markets?
- Which factors are associated with changes in total profit?
- How do product categories compare on unit price and tax?

## 📌 Key Findings Reported

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Market | United States is reported as the dominant market and customer base |
| Brand portfolio | Ten brands have approximately equal sales shares (~9.7%–10.2%) |
| Payment | Credit card is the most frequently used payment method (35.04%) |
| Operations | Average shipping cost is tightly clustered at Rs. 7.33–Rs. 7.46 across countries |
| Profit drivers | The report's Key Influencers visual identifies quantity sold as the strongest listed driver |
| Orders | Order volume dips in 2021, recovers in 2022–2023, then softens in 2024 |
