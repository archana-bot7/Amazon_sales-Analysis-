# Amazon Sales Data Schema

| Field group | Columns |
|---|---|
| Order & customer | `OrderID`, `OrderDate`, `CustomerID`, `CustomerName`, `OrderStatus` |
| Product | `ProductID`, `ProductName`, `Category`, `Brand` |
| Transaction values | `Quantity`, `UnitPrice`, `Discount`, `Tax`, `ShippingCost`, `TotalAmount` |
| Payment & geography | `PaymentMethod`, `City`, `State`, `Country`, `SellerID` |
